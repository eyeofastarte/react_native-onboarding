# Focus Bear Product Reflection

Using Focus Bear is a big help for studying, it's like having a study partner that is always available. It helped me a lot in my study because the experience is calm and supportive instead of stressful. Even though I mostly use the timer feature, I still found the app much better than using a normal phone timer and notes as it ties all of your work habits into one place.

## Reflection Questions

- **What did you learn about the product that you didn't know before?** I learned that Focus Bear is more than a focus app, it is designed as a full lifestyle improvement environment that helps blocking out distractions and improving good habits.
- **Did you spot anything in the help center that seems out of date or inconsistent with the current app?** I did not notice any out-of-date content or breaking bugs during this review.

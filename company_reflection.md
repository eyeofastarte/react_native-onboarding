## Why do you think Focus Bear was created?
Focus Bear was created to bring awareness to both ADHD and neurotypical people about how our brains really work, while helping them on their learning and productivity journey. It's far more effective to build one strong tool that can support thousands than trying to help people one-on-one. The Founder Jeremy who was late-diagnosed with ADHD, drew from his own experiences overcoming heavy gaming addiction and later turning that same intensity into work addiction. He has poured that life knowledge into a practical focused app designed for neurodivergent brains. Beyond the tool itself, Focus Bear builds a community where people can talk about their struggles and get support. For those with the social skills to engage, it's powerful. For those who don't, it still helps in building structure and accountability.

## What problem is Focus Bear solving?
Focus Bear is working on solving the core challenges in productivity, impulsive decision-making, and social/communication gaps. A challenge many students face is knowing what you should do but struggling to start or stick with it. It helps manage constant distractions on my phone and keep track of my study progress. The app builds on techniques like the Pomodoro method but adapts them specifically for AuDHD brains.
The smart blocking and real-time guidance features directly tackle impulsivity, while the wider community on Discord and university campus spaces provides extended support for discussing and addressing these problems.
Focus Bear doesn't magically fix everything, but it provides the external scaffolding needed to follow through on plans, build consistency, and avoid the standard burnout that comes from unchecked hyperfocus or chronic under stimulation.

## Why is this mission important?
Improving focus and helping students succeed is a significant foundation, it directly addresses the larger issues that ADHD and autism create around focus, productivity and long-term employment. These challenges often lead to higher unemployment rates, skill regression, and significant mental health strain. The fact that Focus Bear was designed by people who have lived through the same struggles gives it a unique, practical insight that traditional tools simply don't have. This lived experience helps the app avoid leaving neurodivergent people feeling more demotivated or lost when standard approaches fail. Focus Bear tries to help people reach their potential instead of constantly fighting their own brain. It also focuses on preventing burnout rather than just telling people to grind harder, which matters for mental health in the long run.





## How does Focus Bear's work align with your personal values or interests?
It aligns with my belief that we should build a fairer world by creating better tools and support for people who've been left behind by systems not designed for them. Traditional approaches often feel like trying to navigate with a map drawn for completely different terrain. It works great for some, but leaves others constantly lost or exhausted trying to follow it.
Focus Bear feels like a practical attempt to create better-fitting tools instead. For me, improving focus goes far beyond studying, it's a deeper life approach. It means honestly examining your environment, relationships, strengths and limitations, and finding ways to work with your brain rather than against it. What resonates with me in the app is breaking down big goals into manageable steps and taking a relaxed approach when you don't complete everything perfectly.
I believe real progress comes from understanding how different brains actually function and building solutions around that truth, rather than expecting everyone to fit the same mould. That's why I'm interested in education technology like this.

## Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?
Having lost an enormous amount of time to social media, I strongly relate. I used to attempt to study but would get stuck, distracted, and abandon studying. After years of self-development, that is no longer the case, but I think a lot of us carry these kinds of internal battles. This is an area the Focus Bear team clearly has a lot of personal experience with and is genuinely working hard to help people overcome. That's why I see this internship as a great opportunity to connect with others, learn from their experiences, and bring people together.
## How might Focus Bear help them (or you)?
I can see how valuable Focus Bear is for many other people. It stands out because it combines distraction blocking with actual habit-building support. After using the app for a week, what I liked most was the daily task checklist. It works much better than my usual messy scattered notes and to-do lists. The feature feels like a simple daily planner, similar to a weekly or work planner but focused on smaller personalized goals that feel realistic and achievable.
Focus Bear seems especially useful for students or anyone still trying to build stronger study habits and techniques. I also like that the company puts real effort into building a supportive community around focus. That makes the whole thing feel better.
I've tried quite a few productivity tools over the years, like Cold Turkey a while back, hiding YouTube thumbnails, and my current extension DistractMe for blocking distractions. Most of them didn't make any lasting difference for me. But after years of studying, testing different systems, and slowly building better habits, my focus is now stronger on its own.

# Professionalism at Focus Bear

## Research & Learn

### Professional vs. unprofessional behaviour in a casual workplace

In a casual environment you still need to act responsibly. Acting professionally would include communicating effectively, coming prepared to work, meeting your deadlines, and highlighting blockers. Unprofessional behaviors may include ignoring messages or missing meetings without notice. A relaxed culture does not excuse dismissing others ideas or dodging responsibility & team members still expect each other to keep their commitments.

### Respectful communication in remote teams

For written communication in a remote environment, be sure you use a polite tone to warmly communicate ideas and tasks. Keep messages concise and questions clear when you ask for help or to discuss a project. When communicating, make sure you assume the best of everyone's intentions and don't assume they mean to be rude so you avoid getting defensive or starting a conflict. When you have a disagreement, be respectful by focusing only on the issue and not on personal feelings. This avoids getting into conflicts or escalating any issues that may arise.

### Giving and receiving feedback professionally

Feedback becomes much easier to process when it targets the work itself instead of the individual. If I need to provide feedback, I find it useful to explain specifically what might be improved and occasionally propose an alternative approach to make the comments more constructive. On the receiving end, listening carefully without becoming defensive is crucial. Treating feedback as an opportunity for improvement rather than personal criticism shifts the whole dynamic. Asking clarifying questions usually helps me grasp the core issue better.

## Reflection

### Great teamwork I have experienced

During a recent university web development project, my group managed to build a highly effective workflow. We kept a shared task board updated to track progress and communicated quite regularly. Team members were comfortable admitting when they hit blockers and asking for help. The project felt significantly less stressful simply because everyone remained organised and treated each other with respect.

### Making communication clear, respectful, and inclusive

Ensuring my messages are easy to understand usually involves being direct while staying polite. Before hitting send, taking a brief moment to reread a message helps me catch anything that might sound harsh or unclear. During conversations, I focus on listening carefully and considering different perspectives before offering my own response.

### Creating a positive, fun, and professional environment

A good workplace balance combines responsibility with a friendly attitude. I aim to contribute by remaining approachable and offering help when teammates need it. Taking time to acknowledge small achievements also goes a long way in keeping the work environment positive.

### Preparing for meetings

Before any meeting, reviewing the agenda and related project documents is my first step. This gives me time to figure out which updates to share or questions to ask. Once the meeting starts, taking reliable notes ensures I do not forget important decisions or my specific action items.

### Being proactive during the internship

Taking initiative is essential so I am not just sitting idle waiting for instructions. Whenever I finish a task, checking what needs prioritising next keeps progress moving. When stuck on a problem, my approach is to spend some time researching and troubleshooting independently. If I still need assistance after that, I can reach out while clearly outlining the steps I have already tried.

### Following up and escalating
When you ask a colleague for something, always first contact them politely by email or chat. Everyone is busy, so it's understandable if responses take a little longer. But if you don't get a response after a reasonable amount of time, send a short reminder to continue the conversation. If the delay is critical to project progress, try another channel of communication or proactively contact a supervisor to clear the obstacle.

### Things I will not do
Some behaviors can lower team productivity or create unnecessary confusion. If you know you're running late, inform your colleagues in advance. Don't miss scheduled meetings without notice, and instead of rejecting or fighting constructive feedback, actively use it to your advantage.

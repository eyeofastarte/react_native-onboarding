# My User Manual

## My Role
I’m a React Native intern currently going through the Focus Bear onboarding program. I work mostly remotely. My usual schedule is Thursday for about 5 hours and Friday for around 5 hours.

## How I Work Best
I do my best work when I have a clear task and a quiet environment to focus in. Having written instructions or notes helps a lot because I can refer back to them while working. I sometimes struggle when instructions are vague or when I have to switch between too many tasks quickly.

## Communication Style
I prefer short, clear written communication, especially through Discord. Quick messages work well for me because they make it easy to keep track of information and respond when I’m available. If something is urgent or blocking progress, a message or tag on Discord is the best way to reach me.

## Collaboration & Teamwork
Clear expectations help me collaborate better with a team. I appreciate it when everyone keeps the task board or tracker updated so the group knows what’s happening. In meetings, I usually take a moment to think through ideas before responding so I can give a more considered answer.

## Focus & Productivity
I stay focused by working in blocks of time and keeping a simple to‑do list. Listening to instrumental music or background noise also helps me concentrate. Frequent notifications, noisy environments, and unclear priorities can make it harder for me to stay focused.
